Join the party

See where everyone in the group is, live on the map in MapAround, online and off-grid over LoRa radios.

Open in MapAround Get MapAround on Google Play Join in your browser
  1. No MapAround yet? Install it from Google Play.
  2. Come back to this page and tap Open in MapAround.
  3. Tap Join in the app.

Open this invite on your Android phone to join, or join here in your browser.

In the browser your position is shared while the page is open (a phone stops pages in the background); in Chrome or Edge it can use a Meshtastic LoRa radio by Bluetooth too. The app shares it with the screen off.

You can also paste the link in MapAround's Party tab. Only people with this link can see the party, so share it only with your group.
