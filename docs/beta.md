# Join the Android beta

MapAround for Android is in a closed beta on Google Play. Anyone can join, in three steps, with the
Google account you use on your phone:

1. **Join the testers' group** on Google Groups: [MapAround testers]({{ site.beta_group }}) →
   *Join group*.
2. **Become a tester** on Google Play:
   [play.google.com/apps/testing/app.maparound](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.maparound)
   → *Become a tester*.
3. **Install MapAround** [from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.maparound).
   It can take a few minutes after step 2 before Google Play offers it.

<p><a class="btn btn-primary" href="{{ site.beta_group }}">Join the testers' group</a></p>

Updates come through Google Play as usual. To leave the beta, open the tester page of step 2 again.

## What helps most

Use it where you hike, ride or drive, and
[report what doesn't work](https://github.com/adam2314/maparound/issues), with your phone's model
and what you did. That's what gets MapAround ready for everyone.

No Android phone? The [web app]({{ '/app/' | relative_url }}) runs in any recent browser, nothing to
install. The iPhone app comes later.
