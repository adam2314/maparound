# Support MapAround

MapAround was born from a love of mapping and of the data behind maps: the paths, springs, ridges
and trails that people everywhere map together in OpenStreetMap. Maps guide us, and they should be
freely available to all.

**MapAround is free, and it will stay free**: open source (GPL-3.0), no ads, no account, no
tracking, no paid features, and the maps cost nothing to download. Donations don't buy anything
extra. They pay for two things that make MapAround better for everyone:

- **A better web experience.** MapAround's own map online in the browser, as the Android app
  already has it, and maps downloaded in the web app with one tap. That needs the maps hosted where
  a browser may read them, which GitHub doesn't allow.
- **Building the offline maps.** Every month the maps everyone downloads are built from
  OpenStreetMap, with marked trails, path difficulty and 4×4 tracks. The biggest countries need
  bigger machines than the free ones they're built on now.

## What it pays for

| What | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A better web experience** | | |
| Hosting the maps for the browser | about $2–18 a month | The maps where the web app may read them (Cloudflare R2): the current ones, and up to a year of earlier ones to fall back on. |
| A domain of its own | about $15 a year | A short address for the website and the web app, instead of adam2314.github.io/maparound. |
| **Building the offline maps** | | |
| Building the biggest countries | about $10–30 a month (estimate) | France, Germany, Great Britain, Poland, the Alps, Japan, India, China, Indonesia, Brazil and the United States are too big for the free build machines; a bigger rented machine for a few hours each month builds them. Until then their regions (states, provinces) can be downloaded. |
| **On the phones** | | |
| Apple developer account | $99 a year | To put MapAround on the iPhone (the App Store and TestFlight). |
| Google Play developer account | $25 once (paid) | To publish on Google Play. |

Altogether about **$25–55 a month**, depending on how much map history is kept and how long the
big countries take to build. The estimates become figures once the first runs show them.

## Other ways to help

- **Test new versions** on your phone and [report what doesn't work](https://github.com/adam2314/maparound/issues),
  with the phone's model and what you did.
- **Improve OpenStreetMap** where you hike or drive: paths, springs, trail colours, how passable a
  road is. MapAround's maps are built from it every month, so your edits reach the app.
- **Translate** the app into your language (it speaks English and Hebrew so far).
- **Tell others** who hike, ride or drive off-road.

The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/adam2314/maparound), and the maps are built in the open
by [my-pipeline](https://github.com/adam2314/my-pipeline).
