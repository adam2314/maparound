MapAround is an offline map for hiking, biking and off-road driving. Download maps of any country, plan a route and follow it with no phone signal, and see where everyone in your group is, even beyond mobile coverage over LoRa radios.

NO SIGNAL NEEDED
• Offline maps of any country, from OpenStreetMap, in a detailed topo style
• Contour lines and hill shading
• Marked hiking trails and cliffs
• Offline search: towns, peaks, springs, streets and paths
• Offline routing for hiking, cycling and driving

PLAN AND NAVIGATE
• Tap points to plan a route, then drag to change it
• Distance, climb, estimated time and elevation profile
• Turn-by-turn navigation with voice prompts. It keeps going with the screen off and finds a new route if you leave it
• Android Auto: the map, your route and navigation on the car screen

RECORD AND SAVE
• Record your tracks with GPS, also with the screen off
• Save places with a category and a note: camp, water, viewpoint, parking, danger…
• Import and export GPX files, and share points and routes as links

TRAVEL AS A GROUP
• Create a party and invite others with a QR code or a link
• See everyone on the map with their own icon and colour
• SOS alerts, a meeting point and short messages
• Works over the internet, and off-grid through Meshtastic LoRa radios paired by Bluetooth. A phone with both passes messages between them
• End-to-end encrypted: only your party can read it

MAPS YOUR WAY
• Topo, Classic and other styles, or import your own mapsforge theme
• Satellite imagery under the offline map's trails and labels
• Online maps: OpenTopoMap, CyclOSM, satellite imagery, national maps (swisstopo, basemap.at, Kartverket, IGN, USGS) and your own tile servers
• Raster maps such as scanned topo sheets: MBTiles, SQLiteDB and TwoNav/CompeGPS .rmap
• 3D view with terrain relief and buildings

TOOLS FOR THE FIELD
• Bearing and distance between two points, true and magnetic
• Line of sight and viewshed: what can be seen from a point
• Radio link planner: Fresnel zone clearance and path loss for LoRa, VHF, PMR446 and more
• Coordinates in decimal degrees, DMS, UTM, MGRS, Israeli ITM, Plus Codes and Maidenhead
• Compass with true north
• Speedometer and trip counter
• Inclinometer for 4x4s (pitch and roll) and a G-meter
• Barometer with altitude and pressure trend
• GPS satellite view with sky plot and signal strength

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your maps, tracks and places stay on your phone. MapAround is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.
