# MapAround guides

Short guides to the data MapAround works with: what it is, where it comes from, how to get it,
the space it takes and what it shares. The **Offline maps** and **Party** screens link to them.

- [Offline maps]({{ '/guide/offline-maps' | relative_url }}): MapAround's monthly OpenStreetMap
  maps, the mapsforge.org maps, and importing `.map` files.
- [Raster maps]({{ '/guide/raster-maps' | relative_url }}): scanned and saved maps (`.rmap`,
  `.mbtiles`, `.sqlitedb`) under or instead of the vector map.
- [Elevation data]({{ '/guide/elevation' | relative_url }}): the heights behind contour lines, hill
  shading, line of sight and the 3D view, and how to use another source.
- [Offline routing]({{ '/guide/offline-routing' | relative_url }}): planning routes on the phone
  with BRouter, without a connection.
- [Marked trails]({{ '/guide/marked-trails' | relative_url }}): waymarked hiking routes, built in,
  downloaded or online.
- [Parties]({{ '/guide/party' | relative_url }}): several parties at once, and each one on the map:
  members, trails, the meeting point and marks, and the connections.
- [Party server]({{ '/guide/party-server' | relative_url }}): how the party's messages travel, what
  servers see, and running your own.
- [The map in your website]({{ '/guide/embed' | relative_url }}): the web app's map on any web
  page, with a track, a GPX file or a point.

More: [map styles]({{ '/map-styles' | relative_url }}) and the
[privacy policy]({{ '/privacy' | relative_url }}).
